CORE ARCHITECTURE

3-Stage Industrial Health & Protection Architecture

Engineered to reject transient false alarms caused by plant floor noise, dropped tools, and motor startup speed ramping without compromising sub-second defect detection.

SAFEGUARD 01

Speed-Ramp Transient Gate

Monitors rotor acceleration during startup (0 to rated RPM). Discards startup inrush current shocks and torque transient spikes during the initial 1.28s window before establishing steady-state baseline tracking.

SAFEGUARD 02

Transient Outlier Shock Shield

Distinguishes between continuous structural bearing degradation and isolated mechanical shocks (e.g. dropped wrenches, pipe fluid hammer, or forklift base bumps). Single-sample outliers are filtered prior to health decay.

SAFEGUARD 03

Epistemic Anti-Poisoning Lock

Prevents baseline corruption. When mechanical defect energy rises above nominal baseline thresholds, adaptation gates instantly clamp to prevent the engine from learning anomalous vibration as "new normal."
